# Northstar Works Annual Leave and Wellbeing Guide

Northstar Works is fictional. This synthetic guide is for demonstration and
is not legal, medical, or employment advice.

## Annual leave

For this fictional example, a full-time employee receives 20 working days of
annual leave per calendar year. New starters receive a prorated allowance
based on their start date. Part-time arrangements are calculated
proportionally by the fictional People Operations team.

Submit a leave request in the fictional People Portal at least five working
days before the first day away. The employee's team manager reviews the
request. Ask fictional People Operations to confirm an individual balance;
this general guide does not contain personal leave records.

## Wellbeing support

Employees may contact fictional People Operations to ask about available
wellbeing resources or request a confidential conversation. This guide does
not describe a real service or emergency procedure.

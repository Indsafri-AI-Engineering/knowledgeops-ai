# Northstar Works Acceptable Use and Information Handling

Northstar Works is fictional. This document is a synthetic demonstration
policy and is not an actual company policy.

## Appropriate use

Use company training systems and work information only for authorized
activities. Keep work accounts for work tasks and use the approved sign-in
page rather than links in unexpected messages.

## Handling information

Share internal work documents only with people who have a business need and
the appropriate access. Do not place passwords, verification codes, API keys,
or private credentials in documents, chat, or support requests. Use the
fictional Northstar Works training portal to find approved procedures.

## Reporting a concern

Report a suspected disclosure, unexpected sign-in request, or lost training
device to the fictional Information Services help desk. Include the
approximate time and a brief description, but never include a password or
authentication code.

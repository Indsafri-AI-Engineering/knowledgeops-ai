# Northstar Works Fictional Recruitment Campaign Process

Northstar Works and this campaign are fictional. This document is a synthetic
workflow example, not a real hiring policy or open job advertisement.

## Example campaign

The fictional Platform Support Analyst campaign asks applicants to
demonstrate clear written communication, basic troubleshooting, and
experience documenting repeatable steps. The fictional Talent Team reviews
applications against the published criteria and records evidence for each
criterion.

## Review safeguards

Screening notes must be relevant to the stated criteria and reviewed by a
human recruiter. A count of profiles that appear to match criteria is not a
recommendation to hire, reject, or rank candidates. Do not infer protected or
sensitive personal attributes.

## Questions

Recruiters should confirm campaign dates, criteria, and process details with
the fictional Talent Team. This document contains no real candidate records
or active requisitions.
